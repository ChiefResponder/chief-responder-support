Chief Responder: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Chief Responder does not collect, share, or sell any personal information.

Your game data

Your department, saved game, and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Premium and Assistant Chief) are handled entirely by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details, name, or Apple Account information.

No tracking or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking, and it includes no third-party software that collects data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be sent to ChiefResponder@proton.me.
