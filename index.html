Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Open How to Play on the title screen, or the Guide inside the game. It explains calls, crews, apparatus, equipment, training, money and expansion.

What is free and what is Premium?

Small Town and Medium Town are free to play. Premium is a single one-time purchase that opens the larger areas (Large Town and up), specialty calls and courses, grants, loans, extra equipment, and more Rope Rescue Lab levels. There is no subscription.

What is Assistant Chief?

Assistant Chief is a separate one-time purchase that adds a longer-serving assistant with automatic maintenance. It works with or without Premium.

I bought something and it is not showing up. How do I restore it?

On the title screen, tap Premium, then Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy it. If it still does not appear, email me.

Where is my saved game stored? Can I move it to a new device?

Your game is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere and does not sync through iCloud, so deleting the app deletes the saved game. Restoring a device from a backup restores the game along with it.

How do I start over?

Tap New Game on the title screen. This replaces your current department, so you will be asked to confirm. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and are not affected.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The game clock or a call seems stuck.

Close the app completely and reopen it. Your game is saved automatically. If it happens again, please email me the details above.
